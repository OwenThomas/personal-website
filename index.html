I work as a statistician, which mostly means I spend my days thinking about uncertainty for a living and occasionally get to say something true about the world because of it.

Outside of that, I write about statistics, literature, history, and culture, and I play music — in a choir, in Irish traditional sessions, and in a band called Unquiet Grind.

This site is a hub rather than a destination — a place to find the research, the writing, and the music, wherever each of them actually lives.
