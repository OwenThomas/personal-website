About

Owen Thomas

Originally from London, I studied Physics at undergraduate and Master's level at the University of Oxford. I changed to Statistics for my doctoral studies, drawn to its general applicability and combination of interesting theory and grounding in observable data.

I moved to Oslo for a postdoc in 2017 and have been mostly based here ever since.

I have broad interests in the arts and humanities outside of the sciences, and am very interested in potential lateral intersections between them. I am chiefly active in literature, music and film, but am capable of becoming interested in more-or-less anything. Please take contact if you have a good idea and would like to collaborate.

Research

My present research interests include generalised Bayesian inference, psychometrics, mental health epidemiology and RCT methods. I have also worked on Bayesian nonparametrics, simulation-based inference, and broadly across clinical research. My present position at OCBE is divided 2:1:1 between my personal research, statistical advising, and RCT analysis with the OUH CTU.

Brief academic topic history:

  • My doctoral work was mainly on method development for scalability and privacy for Gaussian Process models.
  • My postdoc was mostly focussed on likelihood-free inference, with some extensions to prior elicitation and generalised Bayesian inference under misspecifiation.
  • At Ahus I worked widely across clinical research, and developed a personal interest in psychometrics, prostate cancer, and health economics.
